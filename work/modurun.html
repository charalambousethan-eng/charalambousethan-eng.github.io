ModuRun

A modular running belt that stays put, so runners can leave the vest at home.

Type
Soft goods, brand
Distance
10 to 30 km
Fit
5th to 95th percentile
Status
Prototype and renders
01 · Problem

Belts bounce, ride up and fit badly

Runners told me bounce was their biggest complaint. 69% preferred a micro-adjust fit, and most carried a phone and keys.

02 · Process

Close to the waist, easy to reach

Load carried close to the waist moves less. I narrowed the design down from three concepts to a slim, organic shape that follows the hip bone.

Modules attach anywhere along the belt: small pockets for keys and gels, a larger one for a phone or rain jacket.

03 · Outcome

ModuRun

A belt and module system with its own brand, costed for manufacture.
