Panic Potato

See it, match it, grab it. A card game that ends in a scramble for the potatoes.

Brief
Set by Big Potato Games
Type
Party game, packaging
Players
2 to 6
Tools
Adobe Express
01 · Brief

Quick to learn, loud to play

Big Potato games are quick to start, social and silly. I looked at Dobble, Snap and Throw Throw Burrito and found a gap: a simple colour-matching game with a safe physical moment.

02 · Process

Three prototypes

Prototype 1 was cardboard and foam peanuts. Testers found it too chaotic, so one player now calls when cards are passed.

Testers also disliked being knocked out, so elimination became stacking forfeit cards: whispering, one hand behind your back, commentating like a sports presenter.

Prototype 2 added potatoes to grab. Staples in the cards caught on fingers, so prototype 3 used printed, laminated cards and squishy foam potatoes.

03 · Packaging

Built for the shelf

Store visits in Brighton showed party games compete on shelf presence. The 100 by 300 mm box gives a tall face for the graphics, and the potatoes stack inside it.

04 · Outcome

Very silly, very funny

The final round of testers described it as "very silly and very funny". The sizzle video explains the game in under two minutes.
