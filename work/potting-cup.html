Potting Cup

A handheld tool that scoops soil, then flips over to press it down.

Type
Handheld product
Context
Start Up Sussex pitch
Tools
CAD, 3D printing
Status
Prototype
01 · Problem

Repotting is messy and hard on the hands

Trowels are narrow, spill soil over the edge of the pot and need a firm grip. For gardeners with arthritis or hand pain, that grip is the hard part.

Add the key finding from your research here.

02 · Process

One tool, two jobs

The cup scoops straight from the compost bag with a raised, curved lip so it pours without spilling. Flipped over, the base presses and levels the soil.

Side handles were sized so most hands can hold it comfortably, and it holds over half a litre so you refill less often.

I printed it in bright orange so it is easy to spot in a flowerbed.

03 · Testing

Four gardeners, four kinds of use

Each tester used the cup in their own pots and beds. It worked well for potting and soft soil; one tester found it could not dig into firm ground, so it is a potting tool, not a digging tool.

04 · Outcome

Ready to pitch

The final design is intended for recycled plastic, such as ocean plastic recovered from fishing nets. I pitched it to Start Up Sussex with a costed launch plan.
