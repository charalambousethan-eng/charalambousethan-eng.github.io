VisNav

In progress

Two haptic wristbands that tell a visually impaired sailor which way to steer.

Type
Wearable and app
Context
Final-year project, Sussex
Users
Visually impaired sailors
Status
In progress
01 · Problem

Holding a course without sight

Visually impaired sailors rely on a sighted crew calling out course corrections. Holding a heading, knowing when to tack and reading wind shifts are the hardest parts.

02 · Research

Asking the people who sail with them

I sent a questionnaire to people who sail with visually impaired sailors and got 45 responses. The most requested solution was vibration or haptics instead of constant speech.

I also interviewed Pete Burke, who has worked with visually impaired sailors for about 25 years.

03 · Process

Left band, right band

Each wrist carries a band. If the boat drifts off the chosen heading, the band on the side to steer towards vibrates. On course, nothing happens.

An Arduino prototype tested timed left and right pulses. A foam prototype showed the first form caught on clothing and was too bulky, so the casing was cut down.
