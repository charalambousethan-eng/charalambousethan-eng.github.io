Vertical Garden

A garden that hangs from the ceiling, for flats with no floor space to spare.

Client
Stanley Black & Decker
Type
Interior product
Build
Full scale, 16 steps
User
Small-flat renters
01 · Problem

No room for plants

In a small flat, plants take up floor and shelf space, light is limited and renters often cannot drill into walls. The ceiling is the one surface nobody uses.

02 · Process

A suspended frame

I designed a timber ceiling frame with trays hung on rods at different heights, then built it at full scale in 16 steps.

03 · Testing

Hung, loaded and lived with

I tested the structure's strength, its scale in a real room and how it fixed to the ceiling. The user felt it was solid and safe, and liked that it used space that was otherwise empty.
